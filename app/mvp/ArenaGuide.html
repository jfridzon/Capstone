How these rankings work

Each score averages one model’s estimated chance of winning over historical questions with every selected feature. Clearing the features includes all eligible questions. The 48 regressions use decisive human votes from April–July 2025. The shortlist requires a recorded official output rate and at least 100 July appearances.

Rates are USD per million tokens and carry their own dates. Your bill depends on both input and output usage.
