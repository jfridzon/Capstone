Transcripción del documento de la asignación (`Phase 0 and Phase1.docx`).

## Phase 0: Problem definition and business need identification

> For each of up to three possible projects that your team has been thinking about, answer the following questions. There should be one response emailed to me with all group members on the page and cc'd on the email.

1. Define the problem or business need. Develop an understanding of the related business processes and what types of data collection are necessary to answer the business problem.
2. Who is the client who would be paying for or sponsoring your work?
3. Do you think you will be able to find and rely upon historical data for the project?
4. Do you think it will be too difficult to find data necessary for your project?
5. Define what you feel would be a successful project or solution to your business problem. Remember to focus on what solutions you are providing to the client.

## Phase 1: Data Availability

> For one of the possible projects that you considered in Phase 0 (if there is a problem with the project, you can pivot to another), do the following:

1. What are you trying to predict or forecast? (If you are not forecasting or predicting something, please speak to me!!)
2. Does the data you have access to include this value explicitly? If yes, explain. If no, are there any proxies for this value that you can generate from your data and possibly from other sources?
3. If the value you are trying to forecast or predict is not available and a proxy is not possible, it seems you will need to pivot!
4. What predictor variables would you like to be able to use for your analytics? Are these variables explicitly included in the data? If no, how many (and which ones) of these variables could be computed based upon a transformation of available data fields? How many (and which ones) of these can be approximated by a proxy that is either explicitly available or based upon some transformation of available data fields?
5. Make sure when you consider using variables for prediction and/or forecasting that the variables in question would be observable at the time when making the decision and not afterwards. If they would only be available afterwards, you will not be able to score your model on new data to make out of sample predictions/forecasts.
