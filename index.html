Which AI model is best for each type of task?

Most companies pick one AI model and use it for everything. We use real human votes to estimate which model wins on which kind of question, and turn that into a simple ranking per task.

View the repository The dataset

The data

LMArena shows a person two anonymous AI answers to the same question and asks which is better. Models are paired at random and names are hidden until after the vote, so the setup works like a randomized experiment.

135,634human votes (Apr to Jul 2025)
48models with 1,000+ votes
11question characteristics
Rall analysis is reproducible

The method

  1. Describe the questionEleven yes/no labels, such as code, math, creative writing and complexity.
  2. One regression per modelFor each of the 48 models, a logistic regression predicts whether it wins, given those labels.
  3. Rank by taskFor any mix of labels, order the models by predicted win probability.
  4. Test on a month it never sawTrain on April to June, then compare against a single general ranking on July.

Next to the 48 regressions, we also fit a Bradley-Terry model with question covariates as a comparison.

Early result

On 20,416 decisive votes from July that the models never saw, the task-aware regressions picked the winner 65.1% of the time, against 64.5% for one general ranking. The margin is modest, which is itself useful: it shows how much of the gap between models depends on the task.

Work in progress. Numbers come from R/ and docs/ in the repository and may change as we add cross-validation and AUC.

What is next

AUC with 5-fold cross-validation, a price table for each model, a top 3 per task that also considers cost, and a small Shiny app where you pick a task and see the recommended models.
